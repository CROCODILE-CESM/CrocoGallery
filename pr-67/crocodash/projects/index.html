CrocoDash Projects

Let’s get hacking! Here are some projects to get you started, whether you have a research question in mind or are still looking for one. Each project stands on its own — start anywhere.

ProjectWhat it’s useful for
Ocean onlyA plain regional MOM6 case — the baseline the other projects build on
CICEAdding sea ice to a regional case — high-latitude domains where ice cover sets the surface fluxes
WW3Coupling surface waves
MARBLAdding ocean biogeochemistry
Kitchen sinkRunning everything
Bering Sea diagnosticsLooking at the output of a coupled MOM6 + CICE + WW3 run: ice edge, wave field, floe size distribution
Tip

Keep the domain small — a few degrees on a side, a few days of simulation. A region you can force and iterate on beats one you can only run one time or just describe.
